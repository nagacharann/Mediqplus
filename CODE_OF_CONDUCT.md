# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want MediQPlus to be a welcoming, harassment-free space for everyone, regardless of age, background, gender identity, experience level, nationality, appearance, religion, or sexual orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests and discussions
- Give and accept feedback graciously
- Assume good intent, and ask when unsure
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language
- Personal or political attacks
- Publishing others' private information, including any real patient data
- Spam or off-topic promotion

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, remove, or reject contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors. Report concerns privately through the contact in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) or via GitHub's report tools.
